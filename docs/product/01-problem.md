# 1. The problem

## Problem statement

Job seekers apply to dozens of roles, and two things eat their time:

1. **Tracking is scattered.** Applications live across spreadsheets, inboxes and job boards, so status, follow-ups and interview dates slip.
2. **Company research is manual and repeated.** Before every interview, candidates spend hours piecing together news, products, values, required skills and salary for one company, and start from zero for the next.

Existing trackers solve the first problem. Almost none help with the second.

## Where this came from

My own job search: I was tracking applications in a spreadsheet and re-doing the same company research before each interview. Dreamplish started as the tool I wanted.

## Target users

Early-career and mid-career job seekers applying to many roles at once, starting with Saudi Arabia and the Gulf (Arabic and English, Saudi salary data).

## Jobs to be done

- **When** I find a job I like, **I want to** know quickly how well I fit and what to adjust in my CV, **so I can** decide whether to apply and apply well.
- **When** I've applied to many jobs, **I want to** see where each one stands without searching my inbox, **so I can** follow up at the right time.
- **When** I get an interview, **I want** a reliable brief on the company and likely questions, **so I can** walk in prepared without spending hours researching.
