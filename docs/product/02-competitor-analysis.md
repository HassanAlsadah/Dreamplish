## Where Dreamplish wins

- **Research is tied to every application.** A sourced company briefing (news, products, values, skills, watch-outs, the role at that company), cached and shared across users. None of the trackers above advertise this.
- **Every fact is cited.** The AI can only use numbered search results, and each claim links to its source. Guessing isn't allowed.
- **Built for the Gulf.** Arabic and English with full right-to-left layout, and salary ranges for the user's roles in Saudi Arabia.
- **Free and private by design.** No paid tier, no ads, no tracking. CVs have personal details removed before they reach the AI.

## Where competitors are ahead (honestly)

Browser job clipper, application autofill, auto-apply, a contacts CRM and mobile apps. Some of these are on the [roadmap](06-roadmap.md); auto-apply is a deliberate non-goal ([PRD](04-solution-and-prd.md)).
