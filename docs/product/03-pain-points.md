# 3. Pain points → features

| # | Pain point | Feature that solves it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I don't know where each application stands." | **Kanban board and list** with custom columns, search and filters; every status change kept in a **timeline** per job |
| 2 | "I forget to follow up." | **Waiting for a reply** list (14+ days without a change) and **Next step** guidance on each job |
| 3 | "Researching each company takes hours, and I redo it every time." | **Company briefing**: news, products, values, in-demand skills, watch-outs and the role, generated once and reused |
| 4 | "AI tools make things up." | Briefings **cite a source for every fact**; tailored CVs only rephrase facts already in the CV and flag any new number |
| 5 | "I don't know if I'm a good fit before applying." | **Check my fit**: paste a posting, get a requirement-by-requirement match score before applying |
| 6 | "Tailoring my CV for each job is slow." | **Tailored CV**, including in the user's own Word design |
| 7 | "I walk into interviews unprepared." | **Interview prep**: likely questions and talking points from the job, CV and briefing |
| 8 | "Updates are buried in my inbox." | **Outlook integration** (read-only) that turns job emails into suggested updates the user approves |
| 9 | "Salary expectations are guesswork in Saudi Arabia." | **Saudi salary ranges** per role in the briefing, with sources |
| 10 | "Most tools aren't made for Arabic speakers." | Full **Arabic interface with right-to-left layout**, plus English |
| 11 | "I don't want my CV used to train AI or sold." | CV details **removed before AI**, no ads or tracking, account deletion, a plain-language privacy policy |
