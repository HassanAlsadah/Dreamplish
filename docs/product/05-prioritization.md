# 5. Prioritization

## MVP: MoSCoW

| Must have | Should have | Could have | Won't have (now) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job tracker and board | Interview prep | Custom board columns | Auto-apply |
| Company briefing with sources | Check my fit (before applying) | Insights panel | LinkedIn / Glassdoor scraping |
| Match & CV score | Outlook integration | Tailored CV in the user's Word design | Paid plans |
| Arabic / RTL and English | Status timeline | Dark mode | Mobile apps |
| Privacy basics (sign-in, data isolation, deletion) | Usage limits | | |

**Why the briefing is a Must:** it's the differentiator ([competitor analysis](02-competitor-analysis.md)). Without it, Dreamplish is one more tracker.

## Next features: RICE

Reach = share of active users likely to use it per month; Impact 0.25–3; Confidence %; Effort in person-weeks. **All values are estimates before launch data**, to be re-scored once real usage exists ([metrics](07-metrics.md)).

| Feature | Reach | Impact | Confidence | Effort | RICE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Follow-up reminder emails | 70% | 2 | 70% | 1 | **98** |
| Export my data | 15% | 1 | 90% | 0.5 | **27** |
| Cover letter generator | 50% | 1 | 60% | 1.5 | **20** |
| Interview calendar (.ics) | 30% | 0.5 | 70% | 1 | **10.5** |
| Browser job clipper | 60% | 1 | 50% | 3 | **10** |

Export my data ranks lower by RICE, but it is pulled forward anyway: it's a privacy right users expect alongside account deletion.
